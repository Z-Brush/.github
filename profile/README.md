**ZBrush** is designed to sculpt detailed characters, creatures and hard-surface models. Key tools include **Brushes**, **Subdivision** and **DynaMesh**.

## 🧱 Modeling and design tools

### Brushes

Shape anatomy and surface detail with sculpting tools.

### Subdivision

Work from large forms to high-resolution features.

### DynaMesh

Restructure sculpt topology during experimentation.

### Polypaint

Apply color directly to high-density models.

### Retopology

Prepare cleaner geometry for other 3D pipelines.

## 🧭 Character sculpting workflow

Block out major forms with brushes, refine the anatomy at higher subdivision levels and use DynaMesh when topology needs rebuilding. Add Polypaint details after the shape reads well.

## 📁 Formats & useful notes

**Typical output:** sculpt files, meshes and texture data. Large subtool counts affect memory use. Exporting a production mesh may require retopology and a separate UV workflow.
